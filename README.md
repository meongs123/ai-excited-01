# The Lighthouse Keeper Called In Sick, So a Moth Took the Shift. — ai-excited-01

A giant moth, glowing amber against a dark teal night sea, has taken over
the lamp room of an empty coastal lighthouse. The keeper is gone, the lamp
is dark — and the moth is the lamp now. Its lit wings throw a warm beam
across the water like a lighthouse sweep, and small fishing boats follow
that light home. One look, one idea, no text, no clutter: a moth doing a
lighthouse's job.

Why this image: the surprise is the swap itself — the brightest thing in
a lighthouse scene is not the lamp, it is a living moth, slightly tilted,
head bowed, wings spread wide enough to light the sea. The beam gives it
momentum, driving the eye from the moth down to the boats. The craft is
clean: fur, wing eyespots and stonework hold up at full size, and at
thumbnail size the moth, the tower and the beam are still the whole story.

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output directory byte for byte — no
network, no randomness, no re-encoding — and refuses to finish unless the
copy checks out: PNG or JPEG magic bytes, size within 1 MiB, dimensions
within 1024×1024, and a sha256 match against the source.
